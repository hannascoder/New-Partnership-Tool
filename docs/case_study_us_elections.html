Recommendations (rule based, for discussion)

  • AI-generated or doctored media (7 fact-checks, e.g. fabricated graphics on election night). Keep a short guide on spotting doctored graphics and AI video ready before election day.
  • Claims about ballots, voting machines and fraud (52 fact-checks). Explain in plain words how counting and certification work, in the languages of the audiences concerned, and where official results are published.
  • Old or misplaced footage and documents (9 fact-checks). Remind audiences to check source and date; point to verified photo and video streams.
  • Claims about candidates, results and politicians (32 fact-checks). The UN is not a party here: stay with neutral, process-focused facts and refer to the national electoral authorities.
