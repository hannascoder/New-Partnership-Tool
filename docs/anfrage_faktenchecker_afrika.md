# Request to African fact-checkers for a data feed (draft, 18.11.2026)

Use for: Africa Check, PesaCheck and other fact-checkers whose websites block automated access (checked 18.11.2026: HTTP 403 or robots.txt).
Replace the [brackets] before sending. Send from a UN address, with the unit's name; do not send the tool's internal links.

**Subject:** Request: a feed or API for your published fact-checks (UN DGC information-integrity monitoring)

Dear [name / "team"],

I work in the UN Department of Global Communications (DGC), Partnerships. We maintain a small internal tool that helps our communications colleagues
see which false and misleading claims are circulating in which countries and whether the UN already has accurate material on the topic.
The tool only works with fact-checks published by independent fact-checkers; we do not judge claims ourselves.

Your work is among the most important sources for countries in Africa, but your website does not allow automated access, so your fact-checks cannot
reach the tool, and Africa is under-represented in what our colleagues see. We would like to ask if you could allow us to read your published fact-checks in one of these ways:

1. an RSS or Atom feed (or the address of one that already exists),
2. your ClaimReview markup / a data export or API, or
3. a written permission to read your site with our crawler (it identifies itself, respects robots.txt, makes at most one request per second, and stops when asked).

What we would use: the headline, the date, the link to your article, the rating, and the language. We would show the headline with a link back to your article and
name you as the source. We would not copy articles, we would not repost your content, and the tool is for internal use by UN communications staff.
If you have conditions (attribution, delay, rate limits, a contact for corrections), please tell us and we will follow them.

It would be a great help if you could tell us whom to contact on the technical side. Thank you for your work on information integrity.

Kind regards,
